## Conflicts/limitations in the supplied edition

These need editorial confirmation in the publication. The dashboard does not invent a new source value to resolve them.

1. **Enrolment:** Figure 4.1 title and legend show KG/Primary/JHS. The surrounding prose misidentifies the levels and Table 2.1 gives primary GER 103.6 and JHS NER 59.3, consistent with the legend. Use the figure's labels; flag the prose for correction.
2. **PPI:** Table 2.1 sector values differ from the detailed, explicitly end-of-year Figure 5.15 and explanatory text. Use the detailed chapter. IIP water in the summary is -1.1%, while the detailed Figure 5.16 gives -2.1%; use the latter.
3. **Forest:** Table 2.1 still refers to an older reserve-share series. Chapter 7 replaces it with 2025 reported area. Twelve regions appear individually; four are not separately reported. Do not infer current-boundary coverage or zero reserves.
4. **Carbon:** p. 97 prose says grassland total 334.0 Mt C, but components 141.2 + 202.8 and the figure total give **344.0 Mt C**. Use the arithmetic and figure. Carbon is a stock, not an annual sequestration rate.
5. **Youth share:** the deep dive uses approximately 30% aged 15–35 in 2025; Chapter 10 separately cites 38.2% and 11.7M in 2021. Do not mix definitions/years. The 0.83 ratio on p. 109 does not follow from 30.0/33.2; the displayed approximate 2025 ratio is 0.90 and is explicitly derived.
6. **Tourism:** the figure gives inbound expenditure GH¢15.42bn and domestic+inbound GH¢22.01bn; Table 2.1 gives GH¢15.18bn for overnight inbound while the detailed text/table round to GH¢15.4bn. Figure counts and sums differ from table/prose by a few persons. Use the explicitly labelled figure totals, keep outbound separate, and do not treat flows as deduplicated unique people.
7. **Fish:** marine + inland + aquaculture components total 722,647 tonnes; the published total is 722,648. Preserve the published total with rounding/revision qualification. Tilapia/catfish sum differs from the aggregate aquaculture series by 267 tonnes, explicitly discussed on p. 43.
8. **Informal trade:** some rounded quarterly values do not reconcile to the centibillion, notably Q4 2024 3.95−3.53 vs reported 0.41. Preserve source balances; test with rounding tolerance. Twelve-month totals are 20.06−18.34=1.72, not the 1.8 obtained from one-decimal rounded prose.
9. **GDP captions:** Figure 5.3 says US$ but its own axis is thousand GH¢, consistent with the text. Use GH¢. Figure 5.2 repeats 2019 for one x-axis label; the yearly sequence identifies the preceding point as 2018. Exact non-oil level series and numeric historical climate baselines cannot be recovered from labelled observations; do not approximate them visually.
10. **Rounding/methods:** age shares total 99.9%. Census migration, projected population and survey measures have different reference populations. Maternal mortality combines GMMR and PHC, so source changes are disclosed rather than treated as one identical method.
